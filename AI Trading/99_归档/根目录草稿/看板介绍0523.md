给设计师的核心重构方向 / Design Brief

中文	English
先做诊断驾驶舱，不做营销页	Build a diagnostic cockpit, not a landing page
顶部显示系统是否可运行	Show system readiness at the top
把“为什么没有信号”作为核心故事线	Make “why no signal” the core narrative
数据覆盖、信号漏斗、拒绝原因要串起来	Connect data coverage, signal funnel, and rejection reasons
表格可保留，但需要摘要卡、排序、筛选和重点提示	Keep tables, but add summary cards, sorting, filters, and callouts
区分项目主口径和第三方库辅助口径	Separate project-primary metrics from third-party auxiliary metrics
Proposal 区域要强调“只读、待审批”	Proposal area should clearly show read-only / pending approval
